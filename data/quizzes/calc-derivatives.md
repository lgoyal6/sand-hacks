# Calculus I: Derivatives

1. [numeric] What is the derivative of f(x) = 3x^2 + 5x - 7 at x = 2?
   answer: 17
   objective: apply the power rule and evaluate a derivative at a point

2. [mcq] If f(x) = sin(x^2), what is f'(x)?
   - a) cos(x^2)
   - b) 2x cos(x^2)  (answer)
   - c) 2x sin(x)
   - d) cos(2x)
   objective: apply the chain rule to a composite function

3. [mcq] A function has f'(c) = 0 and f''(c) > 0. What happens at x = c?
   - a) A local maximum
   - b) A local minimum  (answer)
   - c) An inflection point
   - d) Nothing can be concluded
   objective: use the second derivative test to classify a critical point

4. [numeric] A ball's height is h(t) = 20t - 5t^2 meters. What is its velocity at t = 1 second, in meters per second?
   answer: 10
   objective: interpret the derivative of position as instantaneous velocity

5. [mcq] What is the derivative of e^x times x?
   - a) e^x
   - b) x e^x
   - c) e^x (x + 1)  (answer)
   - d) e^x / x
   objective: apply the product rule

6. [mcq] Which statement about a function differentiable at x = a is always true?
   - a) It has a maximum at a
   - b) It is continuous at a  (answer)
   - c) Its second derivative exists at a
   - d) Its derivative is zero at a
   objective: differentiability implies continuity
