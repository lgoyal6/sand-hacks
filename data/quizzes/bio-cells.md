# Biology: Cells and Energy

1. [mcq] Which organelle is the main site of ATP production in aerobic respiration?
   - a) Ribosome
   - b) Golgi apparatus
   - c) Mitochondrion  (answer)
   - d) Lysosome
   objective: identify where most cellular ATP is made

2. [mcq] A cell is placed in a solution with a higher solute concentration than its cytoplasm. What happens to the water?
   - a) Water moves into the cell
   - b) Water moves out of the cell  (answer)
   - c) No water moves
   - d) Solutes move in and water stays
   objective: predict osmosis in a hypertonic solution

3. [multi] Which are found in both plant and animal cells? Select all that apply.
   - a) Cell membrane  (answer)
   - b) Chloroplasts
   - c) Mitochondria  (answer)
   - d) Ribosomes  (answer)
   objective: compare structures shared by plant and animal cells

4. [mcq] An enzyme is heated far above its optimal temperature and stops working. Why?
   - a) It runs out of substrate
   - b) Its shape changes and the active site no longer fits the substrate  (answer)
   - c) It becomes a different enzyme
   - d) It speeds up too much
   objective: explain enzyme denaturation through active-site shape

5. [numeric] A human body cell has 46 chromosomes. How many chromosomes are in each cell produced by meiosis?
   answer: 23
   objective: relate meiosis to halving the chromosome number

6. [mcq] What are the main products of photosynthesis?
   - a) Carbon dioxide and water
   - b) Glucose and oxygen  (answer)
   - c) ATP and carbon dioxide
   - d) Oxygen and carbon dioxide
   objective: identify the outputs of photosynthesis
