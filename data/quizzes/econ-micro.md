# Microeconomics: Supply and Demand

1. [mcq] The price of coffee rises. What usually happens to the quantity of coffee demanded?
   - a) It rises
   - b) It falls  (answer)
   - c) It stays the same
   - d) Demand shifts right
   objective: apply the law of demand as a movement along the curve

2. [mcq] A new technology cuts the cost of producing laptops. What happens in the laptop market?
   - a) Supply shifts left, price rises
   - b) Supply shifts right, price falls  (answer)
   - c) Demand shifts right, price rises
   - d) Nothing changes
   objective: predict the effect of a supply shift on equilibrium price

3. [numeric] A price rises by 10% and quantity demanded falls by 20%. What is the absolute value of the price elasticity of demand?
   answer: 2
   objective: compute price elasticity of demand from percentage changes

4. [mcq] The government sets a binding price ceiling below equilibrium. What is the result?
   - a) A surplus
   - b) A shortage  (answer)
   - c) No effect
   - d) Higher prices
   objective: predict the effect of a binding price ceiling

5. [mcq] You skip a $20 concert to work a shift that pays $50. What is the opportunity cost of working?
   - a) $20
   - b) $50
   - c) The concert you gave up  (answer)
   - d) $30
   objective: identify opportunity cost as the next best alternative forgone

6. [multi] Which would shift the demand curve for electric cars to the right? Select all that apply.
   - a) Gas prices rise  (answer)
   - b) The price of electric cars falls
   - c) A tax credit for buying electric cars  (answer)
   - d) Consumer incomes rise, if electric cars are a normal good  (answer)
   objective: distinguish demand shifters from a change in the good's own price
